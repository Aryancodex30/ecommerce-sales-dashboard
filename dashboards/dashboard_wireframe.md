# Dashboard wireframe

## Page 1: Executive Overview
- KPI cards
  - Total Revenue
  - Total Orders
  - Average Order Value
  - Profit Margin
  - Return Rate
- Trend line: Monthly revenue
- Bar chart: Revenue by category
- Map: Sales by region/country
- Table: Top 10 products by revenue

## Page 2: Revenue Analysis
- Area chart: Revenue over time
- Stacked bar: Category revenue by month
- Scatter plot: Order value vs customer segment
- KPI: YoY revenue growth
- Filters: date, region, category

## Page 3: Product Performance
- Horizontal bar chart: Revenue by product
- Heatmap: Category vs margin
- Column chart: Units sold by product
- Table: Stock levels and reorder thresholds
- Product rating trend

## Page 4: Customer Analytics
- RFM-style segment distribution
- Bar chart: Spending by customer segment
- Cohort retention chart
- New vs returning customer comparison
- LTV view by segment

## Page 5: Fulfillment and Returns
- Donut chart: Order status distribution
- Bar chart: Return reasons
- Delivery time by shipping method
- Payment method distribution
- Funnel: Orders -> Shipped -> Delivered -> Returned

## Suggested design
- Theme: Blue/teal with green profit accents
- Keep high-level KPIs visible on every page
- Use slicers for date, region, category, customer segment, shipping method
